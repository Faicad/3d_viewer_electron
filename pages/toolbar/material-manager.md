# Material Manager

Click the **Material Manager** button in the toolbar to open the material editing panel and view/edit material properties of the current model.

Supports adjusting the following parameters:

- **Color** — Modify the base color of the material
- **Metalness** — Control the metallic质感 of the material
- **Roughness** — Control the surface smoothness
- **Opacity** — Adjust material transparency

![](/screenshots/toolbar/en/material-manager-panel.png)

All adjustments take effect in real-time for quick material preview.
