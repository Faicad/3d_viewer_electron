# Perspective Mode

Click the **Perspective/Orthographic** button in the toolbar to toggle between perspective and orthographic projection.

## Perspective Mode

Simulates human eye perspective — objects farther away appear smaller. Ideal for observing depth and spatial relationships.

![](/screenshots/toolbar/en/perspective-mode.png)

## Orthographic Mode

Eliminates perspective distortion — all objects appear at the same scale regardless of distance. Suitable for checking dimensions and alignment.

![](/screenshots/toolbar/en/orthographic-mode.png)
