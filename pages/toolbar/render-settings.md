# Render Settings

Click the **Render Settings** button in the toolbar to open the render parameters panel and adjust scene display effects.

Adjustable parameters include:

- **Environment Map** — Switch different HDR environment maps for varied lighting
- **Wireframe Overlay** — Toggle wireframe display overlay
- **Background Color** — Modify the viewport background color
- **Shadows** — Enable or disable shadow rendering

![](/screenshots/toolbar/en/render-settings-panel.png)

Changes are reflected in the viewport in real-time.
