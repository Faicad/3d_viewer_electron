# Sidebar Control

The toolbar provides **Left Panel** and **Right Panel** buttons to independently toggle the visibility of the left and right sidebars.

## Left Panel

Shows the Scene Tree with the model's hierarchical structure.

Click to toggle visibility:

![](/screenshots/toolbar/en/sidebar-left-visible.png)

Hidden to free more viewport space:

![](/screenshots/toolbar/en/sidebar-left-hidden.png)

## Right Panel

Shows the file list and model information.

Click to toggle visibility:

![](/screenshots/toolbar/en/sidebar-right-visible.png)

![](/screenshots/toolbar/en/sidebar-right-hidden.png)

Both panels are independently controlled for flexible workspace layout.
